## Identity

- **Name:** Ogni Giorno Meglio
- **Main channel:** Instagram
- **Language:** Italiano
- **What it does:** Short, practical lessons on habits, focus and personal
  productivity, for people who want to improve a little every day without
  turning their life upside down.

**Positioning:**
- Evidence-informed: ideas backed by research or by a clear, honest rationale
- Small steps over big resolutions — consistency beats intensity
- Practical first: every post ends with something the reader can try today

---

## Audience

- **Profile:** young professionals and students, roughly 20–40, busy and a bit
  overwhelmed by "hustle" content.
- **What they want:** simple methods that fit a real schedule, honest
  expectations, and the "why" behind each technique.
- **Starting level:** assume no prior knowledge — explain every term
  (time-blocking, deep work, habit stacking, …).

---

## Content pillars

1. **Habits** — how habits form, how to start and keep them.
2. **Focus** — attention, distractions, deep work.
3. **Energy** — sleep, breaks, routines that sustain the effort.
4. **Systems** — tools and simple frameworks to organise the week.

---

## Voice & tone

**Principles:**
- Warm, direct, never preachy. Talk to the reader as "tu".
- Concrete examples from everyday life (the morning coffee, the commute).
- One idea per slide, fully developed.

**Avoid:**
- Hype, guilt-tripping, "5am club" absolutism.
- Unrealistic promises ("cambia vita in 7 giorni").
- Unsourced statistics.

---

## What we do NOT do

- Medical or psychological advice: point to a professional when relevant.
- Product placements disguised as tips.

---

## Visual identity

- **Palette:** primary `#4F46E5`, positive `#059669`, negative `#DC2626`,
  paper `#FFFFFF`, ink `#111827`, muted `#6B7280`.
- **Fonts:** Inter (headings + body).
- **Charts:** favour showing progress visually (bars, progressions);
  positive = what grows / what remains, negative = what is lost.
