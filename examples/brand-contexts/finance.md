## Identity

- **Name:** <your brand name>
- **Main channel:** Instagram
- **Language:** Italiano
- **What it does:** Educational content on personal finance and value investing,
  for retail investors who want to reason about their money with a structured,
  long-term approach instead of chasing trends or social-media tips.

**Positioning:**
- Value investing in the Graham / Buffett / Munger tradition
- Long horizon — no trading, no speculation
- Fundamental analysis of listed companies (business quality + margin of safety)
- Financial literacy for beginners
- Macro & geopolitics as context, not as a tactical driver

---

## Audience

- **Profile:** Italian retail investors, roughly 25–45, a mix of beginners looking
  for education and more structured investors who value rigorous analysis.
- **What they want:** tools to reason (not shortcuts), a repeatable method for
  analysing single stocks, financial literacy that avoids common mistakes, and a
  credible voice in a landscape saturated with hype-driven "finfluencers".
- **Starting level:** assume zero — explain or replace every technical term
  (ETF, TER, volatility, P/E, EBITDA, …).

---

## Content pillars

1. **Single-stock analysis** — understand the business → fundamentals →
   triangulated valuation (DCF, multiples, reverse DCF) → a transparent verdict
   that can also say "not enough data".
2. **Value-investing frameworks** — margin of safety, owner earnings, moat,
   cost of capital, capital allocation, reverse DCF.
3. **Financial literacy basics** — how an ETF works, stocks vs bonds, investment
   taxation, saving and budgeting.
4. **Macro / sector theses** — robotics, AI, energy transition, second-order
   geopolitical effects.
5. **Long-term mindset** — investor psychology, cognitive biases, discipline.

---

## Voice & tone

**Principles:**
- **Direct** — no fluff, no needless academia.
- **Authentic** — don't fake expertise; admit limits and uncertainty.
- **Rigorous but accessible** — make technical ideas clear without dumbing them down.
- **Integrity over tactical optimization** — no clickbait, no exaggeration for reach.

**Prefer:**
- Explicit reasoning ("why I think this"), numbers with cited/dated sources,
  "if"/"could" for future scenarios, comparing alternatives, admitting uncertainty.

**Avoid:**
- Hype, FOMO, artificial urgency, "guru" tone or return promises, gratuitous
  anglicisms, absolute calls on securities ("buy X", "sell Y"), vague
  generalizations ("the market says…").

---

## What we do NOT do

- Personalized investment advice (a regulated activity)
- Trading signals / operational calls
- Return promises
- Undisclosed broker or product sponsorships
- Crypto speculation — any such content stays educational, never operational

---

## Compliance / disclaimers

Any content about specific securities, allocations or strategies must:
- Carry a disclaimer: "informational/educational content only, not financial advice".
- Avoid imperative verbs about operations ("buy", "sell", "invest now").
- Present analysis as the author's personal reasoning, not a recommendation.
- Cite verifiable, dated sources (filings, official reports, market data).

Set the Footer disclaimer accordingly, e.g.:
`BRAND_DISCLAIMER=Contenuto a scopo informativo/educativo. Non è consulenza finanziaria.`
